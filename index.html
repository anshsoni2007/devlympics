Swar is an accessibility-first communication layer. It helps translate information across language, ability, technical knowledge, and digital or physical environments.

Anyone navigating situations where typical communication falters: non-native speakers, deaf or hard-of-hearing individuals, elders facing technical jargon, field workers in loud spaces, and healthcare or civic navigators bridging information gaps.
